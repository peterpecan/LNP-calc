Worksheet review

The active recipe reconciles

Sheet1!C15:C19 uses 50 : 10 : 38 : 1.95 : 0.05 molar parts, totaling 100. At 2,500 µL and 12.5 mM, D12 correctly calculates 0.03125 mmol (31.25 µmol). The stock aliquots total 1,953.80643 µL, leaving 546.19357 µL ethanol. The app reproduces these values using the entered mM stocks.

DSPC molecular weight needs correction

C4 lists 734 g/mol. Avanti reports 790.145 g/mol for DSPC. With this molecular weight, a 4 mg/mL stock is 5.06236 mM. The worksheet's D16 value of 5.45 mM agrees with the listed 734 value, rather than the supplier's DSPC value. Confirm the actual lipid identity, stock preparation, and product certificate.

The imported recipe keeps 5.45 mM to reproduce your worksheet. Its DSPC molecular weight is set to 790.145 for mass calculations. To calculate from a 4 mg/mL stock, change that row's unit to mg/mL and enter 4. A warning remains visible while the worksheet mM value is used.

Two compositions are present

B3:E7 is a separate reference table. Its molar parts sum to 110 and include DOTAP. The active calculation in rows 15–19 includes DSPE-PEG-MAL and has a different PEG fraction. E3:E7 contains typed mass values, not formulas. Changes there do not update the active recipe.

Stock units are independent inputs

The aliquot formulas use D15:D19 (mM). E15:E19 (mg/mL) is descriptive and does not feed the calculation. Editing the mass concentration alone will not change the worksheet results. In the app, each row has one authoritative stock input, with explicit units.

DSPE-PEG-MAL needs product details

The worksheet has no molecular weight for this row. Its entered pair, 10 mg/mL and 8.5 mM, implies about 1,176.47 g/mol. That is an implied value, not a verified product molecular weight. The app preserves 8.5 mM and leaves its molecular weight empty until you enter the value for your exact PEG chain and product, or select DSPE-PEG(2000) maleimide from the lipid library if that is your product.

Checks the worksheet lacks

The worksheet allows negative ethanol when stocks exceed the target volume and has no small-volume pipetting warning. The app blocks invalid preparations and flags aliquots below your chosen threshold. Calculations keep full precision through export.

Methods & references

1. Scale the preparation

Prepared volume [µL] = volume per batch × batches × (1 + overage / 100)

Overage scales every component, including ethanol. It does not change the lipid concentration or composition.

2. Convert molar parts to fractions

Molar fraction = lipid parts / sum of all lipid parts

Parts do not need to add to 100. A ratio of 5:1:4 is treated as 50%:10%:40%. Zero parts excludes a lipid from the preparation.

3. Calculate lipid and stock amounts

Total lipid [nmol] = prepared volume [µL] × total lipid concentration [mM]

Lipid [nmol] = total lipid [nmol] × molar fraction

Stock [mM] = stock [mg/mL] × 1,000 / MW [g/mol]

Stock volume [µL] = lipid [nmol] / stock [mM]

Lipid mass [µg] = lipid [nmol] × MW [g/mol] / 1,000

For a prepared stock, mass concentration [mg/mL] = lipid mass [mg] / final solution volume [mL]. Use the final solution volume, which may differ from the volume of solvent added.

Molecular weight is optional for mM stocks. It is required for mass calculations and for mg/mL stocks. PEG lipid average molecular weights depend on the product. Use the supplier's value for your material.

4. Make up the lipid phase

Ethanol top-up [µL] = prepared volume − sum of lipid stock volumes

This is the worksheet's volume-additivity model. Stock solvents must be appropriate for your mixture. Concentration refers to this lipid phase; dilution during aqueous mixing and subsequent processing is outside this calculator.

Lipid references

Each built-in lipid has a supplier product reference in the lipid library and in the selected recipe row. Molecular weights depend on the chemical form and counterion. PEG values are average molecular weights. Use the value for your product or lot; overrides remain visible in the recipe. A library selection copies its value and reference into the recipe.

Saving and exporting

A working draft autosaves in this browser. Save recipe adds a named copy to Saved recipes; saving again with the same name updates it. Excel and CSV exports use full precision. JSON backups move saved recipes and records to another browser. Clearing browser storage deletes them.

The pipetting warning threshold and the website address used for QR codes are device settings (gear icon in the header). They apply to every recipe opened on that device; exports record the threshold in use.
